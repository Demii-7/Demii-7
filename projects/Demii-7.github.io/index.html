What Do Spectrum Prediction Models Actually Learn?

Spectrum prediction models are often evaluated using forecasting accuracy alone. This work uses a common benchmark across multiple wireless research testbeds to examine whether strong predictive performance actually reflects meaningful temporal, spectral, and spatial learning, and whether these models generalize across environments.

Research question

To what extent do spectrum prediction models learn meaningful patterns?
